# Golden Brief 006 — Event marketing campaign

**Campaign Name:** RevOps Summit 2026

**Business Objective:** Drive 800 qualified registrations for the virtual RevOps Summit on October 14, 2026, with at least 60% attendance on the day.

**Target Audience:** Heads of Revenue Operations, Sales Operations Directors, and Demand Gen leaders at B2B SaaS companies, North America and EMEA.

**Key Message:** Six revenue operations leaders walk through the exact playbooks they used to cut Monday-morning reconciliation in half, with a 30-minute live Q&A.

**Channels:** Email, LinkedIn, Event Page, Paid Search

**Budget:** $65,000 total

**Timeline:** Promotion live August 18, 2026; event October 14, 2026; recordings released October 21, 2026

**Success Metrics:**
- Qualified registrations (target: 800)
- Attendance rate on the day (target: 60%)
- Post-event demo requests (target: 75 in 4 weeks)

**Constraints / Brand Notes:** Use "AI-assisted" not "AI-powered". Avoid "world-class", "leverage", "seamless". Speakers' names + headshots must be on the registration page. Tone: invitational, specific, confident.
