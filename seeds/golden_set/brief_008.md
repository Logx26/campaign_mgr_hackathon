# Golden Brief 008 — Re-engagement / churn prevention

**Campaign Name:** Q3 Dormant Account Re-engagement

**Business Objective:** Re-engage 500 dormant enterprise accounts (no login in 60+ days) and recover at least 80 to active status in 6 weeks.

**Target Audience:** Enterprise accounts (1000+ employees) that have been Axion customers for 12+ months but have had no admin login in 60+ days. Primary contacts are existing champions in revenue operations or sales operations roles.

**Key Message:** You haven't logged in for a while — here's the one report that shows what's changed in your team's revenue picture in your absence, and a single click to get back to the data.

**Channels:** Email, SDR Outreach

**Budget:** $25,000 total

**Timeline:** Live July 15, 2026 through August 26, 2026

**Success Metrics:**
- Reactivation rate (baseline: 9%, target: 16% over 6 weeks)
- Demo refresh requests (target: 40 in 6 weeks)

**Constraints / Brand Notes:** Do not reference the word "churn" externally. Use "AI-assisted" not "AI-powered". Each email must reference the customer's connected data sources by name. Tone: helpful, low-pressure.
