# Golden Brief 004 — Thought leadership / content marketing

**Campaign Name:** State of Revenue Operations 2026

**Business Objective:** Establish Axion as the authoritative voice on revenue operations by publishing the "State of RevOps 2026" report. Drive 5,000 report downloads and 250 demo requests within 12 weeks of publication.

**Target Audience:** Heads of Revenue Operations, VPs of Sales, Chief Revenue Officers at B2B SaaS, fintech, and martech companies with 100–5000 employees.

**Key Message:** The State of Revenue Operations 2026 quantifies what most teams suspect — Monday morning reconciliation costs the average B2B company $360,000 per year per team. The report includes benchmarks, the data behind them, and the playbook seven peers used to cut that cost in half.

**Channels:** LinkedIn, Email, Landing Page

**Budget:** $95,000 total

**Timeline:** Report ready by August 4, 2026; promotion live August 11, 2026 through November 3, 2026

**Success Metrics:**
- Report downloads (target: 5,000 in 12 weeks)
- Demo requests attributed to the report (target: 250)
- Inbound press mentions (target: 8 in 12 weeks)

**Constraints / Brand Notes:** Cite proprietary data only — no scraped statistics. Do not name competitors. Avoid superlatives ("world-class", "industry-leading"). Use "AI-assisted" not "AI-powered". Tone: confident, data-led, never preachy.
