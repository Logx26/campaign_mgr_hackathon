# Golden Brief 002 — Complete B2B SaaS launch

**Campaign Name:** Axion Insights GA Launch

**Business Objective:** Generate 80 product-qualified leads for the new Insights module within 8 weeks of GA launch. Secondary: drive 200 demo requests from existing trial accounts.

**Target Audience:** Heads of Revenue Operations and Directors of Sales Operations at B2B SaaS companies with 250–2000 employees in North America. Decision-maker titles include VP RevOps, Director of Sales Operations.

**Key Message:** Axion Insights replaces the Monday-morning reconciliation cycle with one decision-ready view. AI-assisted reporting flags the three accounts most likely to convert this week, with the supporting data behind every claim.

**Channels:** LinkedIn, Email, Paid Search

**Budget:** $220,000 total

**Timeline:** Campaign live September 9, 2026; assets locked August 30, 2026

**Success Metrics:**
- Product-qualified leads (baseline: 35/quarter, target: 80 in 8 weeks)
- Demo requests (baseline: 120/quarter, target: 200 in 8 weeks)
- Trial-to-paid conversion (baseline: 14%, target: 22%)

**Constraints / Brand Notes:** Do not reference competitors by name. All time-savings claims must cite a customer story. Avoid "AI-powered" — use "AI-assisted." Avoid "revolutionary" and "seamless." Tone: confident, specific, never aggressive.
