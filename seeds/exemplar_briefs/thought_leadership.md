# Sample Brief D — Thought Leadership / Demand Generation

**Campaign Name:** "The Forecast Honesty" Thought Leadership Series

**Business Objective:** Build awareness with 10,000 net-new RevOps and Sales-leader contacts via a long-form report + 8-week content cadence. Secondary: drive 300 sign-ups to the report download form (gated).

**Target Audience:** RevOps Directors, VP-Sales, and CROs at companies with 200+ employees. Both customers and non-customers.

**Key Message:** The way most teams forecast is broken not because of the data, but because of the meeting. The Friday pipeline review burns hours and gives leaders a number they can't actually act on.

**Channels:** LinkedIn (organic + paid), Email (existing list nurture), Landing Page (gated report download), Event Page (1 launch event), SDR Outreach (post-download follow-up)

**Budget:** $65,000 total. Report production (writer + designer): $18,000. Paid LinkedIn: $25,000. Event production: $12,000. Landing page + creative: $10,000.

**Timeline:** Report drops November 6. 8-week content cadence runs October 16 – December 11. Launch event November 13.

**Success Metrics:** Net-new contacts (target: 10,000 in 8 weeks); report downloads (target: 300); event registrants (target: 250); subsequent demo requests (secondary metric, no target).

**Constraints / Brand Notes:** Report must include 3+ original data points (not industry-survey rehash). Speakers and guests must reflect the audience demographic. Tone: contrarian-but-credible. Do not name competitors. Do not gate the executive summary — only the full report is gated.
