# Sample Brief E — Product Update / Feature Announcement

**Campaign Name:** Smart Alerts v2 — Activation Push

**Business Objective:** Drive 35% of existing customers to enable the new Smart Alerts v2 feature within 30 days. Secondary: collect 25 customer feedback responses for a v3 roadmap.

**Target Audience:** Existing customer admins (CSM-managed accounts only). Roles: RevOps lead, Sales-Ops lead, CS-Ops lead. Active users in last 30 days.

**Key Message:** v2 lets you set alerts in the language your team already uses ("any deal stalled > 5 days"). No more SQL. No more JSON paths. Just sentences.

**Channels:** Email (in-product nurture), Product In-App Banner, Webinar (1 launch session), CSM-led one-to-one outreach (top 50 accounts).

**Budget:** $12,000 total. Email production: $2,000. Webinar production: $5,000. CSM enablement materials: $3,000. Creative: $2,000.

**Timeline:** Activation push runs October 21 – November 20. Webinar October 28. CSM outreach concludes November 7.

**Success Metrics:** Smart Alerts v2 activation rate among CSM-managed cohort (baseline: 0%, target: 35% within 30 days); webinar attendance (target: 150 customers); feedback responses (target: 25).

**Constraints / Brand Notes:** Do not message non-CSM-managed self-serve customers (different rollout). Tone: helpful, "we built this for you." Show before/after visuals of the new alert builder. No "click here" CTAs.
