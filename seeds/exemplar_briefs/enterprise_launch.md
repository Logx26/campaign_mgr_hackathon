# Sample Brief C — Enterprise Product Launch

**Campaign Name:** RevPilot Launch — Enterprise GA

**Business Objective:** Generate 75 enterprise (5000+ employee) demo requests in 8 weeks for the GA launch of RevPilot. Secondary: secure 10 customer-story commitments from beta users.

**Target Audience:** VP-Sales, VP-RevOps, and CRO at enterprise companies in financial services, healthcare, and software. Existing CRM users (Salesforce or HubSpot Enterprise).

**Key Message:** Forecasts shouldn't read like quarterly fiction. RevPilot tells your sales leaders what's actually happening in pipeline — every day, not every Friday.

**Channels:** LinkedIn, Email (executive sequence), Event Page (3 webinars), SDR Outreach (named-account list), Landing Page (gated playbook)

**Budget:** $120,000 total. LinkedIn paid: $40,000. Email production + sends: $8,000. Event production (3 webinars + speakers): $35,000. SDR enablement + lists: $15,000. Creative + landing page: $22,000.

**Timeline:** Launch September 9. Webinar 1: September 16 (CRO panel). Webinar 2: September 30 (RevOps deep-dive). Webinar 3: October 14 (customer panel). All assets locked August 30.

**Success Metrics:** Enterprise demo requests (target: 75 in 8 weeks); webinar attendance (target: 200 net-new enterprise attendees across 3); customer story commitments (target: 10).

**Constraints / Brand Notes:** Customer stories must have signed approval before publication. Webinars must include a non-promotional 20-min substantive segment. Do not reference Salesforce or HubSpot in copy except in factual integration claims. Tone: senior, candid, slightly contrarian — speak to executives who've sat through too many vendor pitches.
